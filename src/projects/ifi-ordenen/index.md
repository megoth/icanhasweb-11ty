Ifi-ordenen is the official website of [Hennes Majestet Keiserpingvinen den Fornemme](https://ordenen.ifi.uio.no/) (Her Majesty the Royal Penguin the Distinguished), a knight order that focuses on honoring people that have accomplished great deeds for the students at [Department of Informatics](https://ifi.uio.no).

The website consists of static HTML-pages that are built using [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/) and [Sanity.io](https://www.sanity.io/).

I've been the main driver behind this project, primarily as developer, but have received help from my fellow members in producing content.
