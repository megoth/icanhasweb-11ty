A song I've made based on a talk Alan Watts did on randomness and coincidences and a recording of Clinamen, an art
installation in France where ceramic bowls of various sizes float in a large pool, and when they collide they create
these lovely sounds (made available thanks to Kevin Luce and freesound.org).

I paired these sounds with a combination of semi-random and non-random beats, and a generative patch on my Oxi Coral.
The song is simple, with small variations, and although there are some mistakes (some "breaking" sounds, high noise,
etc) sometimes, I hope you enjoy it ^_^
