The result from a session yesterday. The Solar 42f kinda stole the show for me, both providing a textural background of
drones, but also an arpeggio that I had a lot of fun tweaking.

I'm also happy with the beat; when I locked in the final groove element using Collide 4 and Rainmaker, I knew I wanted
to turn this into something I could upload and share.

There's also a generative patch, created using the Oxi One and setting probability for various notes, that goes through
most of the song. It's a bit weird, but I find it somewhat charming, and creates more movement.

Finally there's Serum 2 providing the long, higher-pitched sounds that come about 1/3-1/2 into the song.

Had fun creating it, hope you like it ^_^
