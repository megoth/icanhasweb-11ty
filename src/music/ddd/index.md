I've been playing around with my Torso S-4 more recently, and have started using it to record some sequences I play on
the Osmosis.

That is how this song came about. It started as a sample of a two dogs barking, tweaked into a somewhat random, ambient
texture, that I then jammed along with on the Osmosis.

In the end the barking became to weird to include in the final version, but such is the process sometimes. I'm happy
with the result ^_^
