I've been playing around on the Elmyra, and experimenting with a technique that produces a rhythmic bass drone. The
result is somewhat random, and I was very pleased when I managed to record the one that made the base for this song ^_^

The Solar 42f comes in with some harmonic drones, and the Chromaplane fills out the mid space a bit more. I also added a
sound from Equator2, to back up the higher notes from Solar.

It's a slow burn, and I recommend letting concepts such as rhythm go =p Enjoy ^_^
