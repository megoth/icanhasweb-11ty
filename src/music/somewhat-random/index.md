Came across an interesting white-noise-ish sound with the Collide 4, and thought it could pair nicely with a generative
sequence that filled the mid to high frequencies.

Rhodes for the End of the World, one of my favorite presets on Equator2, filled the space nicely with some granular
goodness. The sequence was programmed on the Oxi One, where I used a multi-track sequencer with tracks of varying length
and notes with probability of hitting, altogether creating a semi-random sequence using specific notes.

The final piece came in the form of a couple of bass-tracks, one using Equator2, another using Serum 2.

It's a slow burner, hope you enjoy it ^_^
