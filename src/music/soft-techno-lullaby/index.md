Was playing around on the Solar 42f, feeding it a sequence from a Turing Machine eurorack module that's been quantized,
and came across a very catchy sequence of notes. Combined with a generative patch that I made using the Oxi One and Oxi
Coral, they became the base for this song.

I added some layers with Serum 2 and Decent Sampler, which contributed with some longer tones.

I had fun recording it, hope you enjoy listening to it ^_^
