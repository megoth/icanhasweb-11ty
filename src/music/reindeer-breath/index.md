Had a lot of fun exploring this song. It started with a sequence that I played around with on the Vhikk X, creating a
metal-like sound. Coupled together with a sample that I recorded from an interview with the artist Snow Raven, where she
demonstrates a technique called Reindeer Breath. I ran it through the S-4, mangling it with lots of random modulations.
It's a bit weird, but I think it works ^_^ I'm also fond of the drums that I assembled on this, a bit simpler than I
usually do, but with enough groove.
