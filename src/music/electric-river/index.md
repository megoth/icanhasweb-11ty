I saw a YouTube video by Andre Bregegere recently where he improvised some sounds on the Torso S-4, and he demonstrated
some techniques that inspired me to make this song.

I'm starting to get more comfortable with my drum/groove machine, a eurorack synth with various modules for drum- and
bass-sounds, and especially enjoy the sample-based parts, which I channel through a Mutant Clap module which acts as a
LPG, creating this chopped-up sounds that ups the overall groove.

The lead sound is just me improvising on top of the "base" sounds. It's was a fun little piece to jam to ^_^
